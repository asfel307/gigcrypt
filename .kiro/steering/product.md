# Product — GigCrypt

## What it is
GigCrypt is a haunted tracker for people who earn from many small
opportunities at once: coding bounties, freelance gigs, and job
applications. One board, every opportunity, every deadline.

## The problem
Gig and bounty hunters juggle opportunities across many platforms
(make.fun, freelance marketplaces, job portals, spreadsheets, chat
messages). Deadlines slip, prizes are forgotten, and nobody sees the
total pipeline value. Existing trackers are built for sales teams, not
for one person chasing many small prizes.

## Who it is for
- Bounty hunters (e.g. make.fun) tracking many entries and deadlines.
- Freelancers juggling proposals and clients.
- Job seekers tracking applications across portals.

## Core promise
Open the app, see in 5 seconds: what is being hunted, what is due
soon, what is overdue ("risen from the dead"), and how much money is
in the pipeline vs. already paid.

## Non-goals (v1)
- No accounts, no server, no tracking, no payments.
- No scraping of bounty platforms (manual entry first; integrations later).
- No mobile app; the web app must work well on a phone browser.
